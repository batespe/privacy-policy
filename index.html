Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026

Thank you for using SimAngle. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the SimAngle mobile application ("the App").

SimAngle does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personally identifiable information.

About SimAngle

SimAngle is a utility application designed for sim racing enthusiasts. The App provides tools including:

  • Field of View (FOV) calculations
  • Triple monitor setup calculations
  • Angle measurement tools
  • Spirit level functionality

Information We Collect

The App does not require account registration and does not request personal details such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Home address
  • Location data

Device Sensors

To provide angle measurement and spirit level functionality, SimAngle may access device sensors such as:

  • Accelerometer
  • Gyroscope

Sensor data is processed locally on your device and is not transmitted to the developer or any third party.

Photos & Sharing

The FOV calculator can generate an image of your setup. If you choose to save it, the App writes the image to your device's photo library; if you choose to share it, the image is handed to the app you select. SimAngle never reads existing photos and does not upload images anywhere.

Data Storage

Any settings or preferences stored by the App remain on your device. SimAngle does not maintain external databases containing user information.

Analytics

SimAngle does not use analytics services to track user activity.

Advertising

SimAngle does not display third-party advertisements.

Data Sharing

Because no personal information is collected, no personal information is sold, shared, rented, or disclosed to third parties.

Children's Privacy

SimAngle does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

Security

Since the App does not collect personal data, risks associated with transmitting personal information are minimized.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect improvements to the App or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page and will become effective immediately upon publication.
